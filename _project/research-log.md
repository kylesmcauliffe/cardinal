# CARDINAL — Research Log

Every specific claim in the manuscript — date, dollar amount, name, address,
quotation — is logged here with its source before it can leave draft status.

**Rules, from `voice.md`:**

- Wikipedia is a navigation tool, never a citation. Follow it to the primary or
  scholarly source and cite that.
- Citations do not appear in the prose. They live here and in `references.bib`.
- If a claim is contested by historians, it is marked `CONTESTED` and handled
  per Voice Guide rule 2: strongest version of each position, uncertainty named,
  Cardinal's reading identified with its reasoning.
- If the evidence is thin, the claim does not carry a chapter.

**Status values:** `VERIFIED` (primary or strong secondary source in hand),
`NEEDS SOURCE` (claim drafted, citation outstanding), `CONTESTED` (genuine
scholarly disagreement), `CUT` (could not be substantiated).
