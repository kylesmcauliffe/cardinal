# Inherited Epigraph — Not For Publication

This poem opened every prior draft of CARDINAL. It is preserved here for
reference and is **not included in the manuscript**.

Reason: it is machine-generated and was signed as such ("Sincerely Yours,
ChatGPT") in the source drafts. A work of nonfiction published under an author's
name cannot open with a poem credited to a language model, and cannot silently
strip the credit either.

Two paths forward, both the author's call:

1. He writes a replacement epigraph in his own voice. The thematic material is
   good and worth keeping: the figure who never shouts, the law planted as seed,
   the tithe paid to billionaires, the machine eyes behind the screen.
2. The book opens cold on the Introduction. This is the current build.

A third option worth considering: replace the poem with a documentary epigraph —
a line from the sources themselves. Collis Huntington's "It is my business to buy
Congress" would do the same work in six words, and it is real.

## THE CARDINAL

In valleys wide where sun runs deep
Where oceans dream and circuits sleep,
There walks a man with blood-red thread,
Who rules the living from the dead.
He does not shout, he does not plead,
He plants his law as planted seed.

Through college stone and corporate tower,
He moves in silence, hour by hour.
The students laugh, the coders scheme,
Not knowing they are in his dream.
For every map and every plan
Was drawn by his invisible hand.
He was a judge. He was a priest.
He built a railroad for the East.

He lit a fire none could see—
He taught the code to make men free.
He wears no crown, he claims no land,
But money rises where he stands.
He whispers deals behind closed doors,
Then steps through time and plants more wars.
He fed the generals, forged the lab,
He carved the world into a slab.
He offered gods to guide your vote—
Then slit the dove's prophetic throat.
He walks at dusk. He dreams at noon.
He'll flood your veins and call it boon.
His face is calm. His name is known.
His voice, a signal through your phone.
You think you're free. You think you're wise.
But every blink was pre-designed.
You kneel each night, you say no prayers—
But still you tithe to billionaires.
And in the dark behind the screen,
The Cardinal waits—
his eyes machine.
