# Surface: Cardinal site

Mode: Read

Audience: a reader opening the Cardinal archive to find an episode, a chapter, a person, a place, or a source.

Job: locate a real document and read it, with its verification status and branch provenance intact.

Constraints: repository text only; dark default; cardinal red; artometrics-style index (large names, few words); icon-first rail; audio paths `ep0N.mp3` with a coming-soon state; Firebase static export.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The archive is a keyed survey sheet of one corridor, indexed the way a report catalog indexes reports. It refuses the cream-paper history template and the stat-card dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Warm-black ground, Stanford cardinal `#8C1515` as one structural rail and as the masthead field, cream ink. Anybody, condensed, for index titles at several sizes. Literata for the reading column. One-stroke icons. A survey title block holds the sheet name, the book line, and the author. Status is a word.

STORY: From the first screen the reader knows this is Cardinal, book 1 of The McAuliffe Codex, the Stanford corridor, and can open The Ledger or the draft. Inside a document they read a calm column and leave by a real next link.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A fixed cardinal rail on the left, eight icon-and-word links, the current one reversed to cream. The masthead is a cardinal title block: the existing cover plate at large scale, the word Cardinal set to the column width, then the book's own sentence "Rail controlled movement. Stanford controls succession." Episode bands — number, icon, name, years — start in that same viewport.

FORM: Brief-pinned artometrics index, translated onto a dark cardinal sheet. Seed da5e2f32 assigned grounded candidate 5 (a survey plat). The brief outranks the roll on palette, darkness, and index form. The plat remains as the title block and as the places plate: a keyed map, legend beside it, unlocated names listed rather than forced onto a pin. Raises kept from the hand: status is spelled, not only colored (cyclorama); the red rail is the single structural member (tensegrity); one display face at many sizes (bitmap specimen); a billing line at the foot of every document (one-sheet); the column does not move (cracktro); the episode you are in is marked and its sources stay on the page (cutting bench).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
