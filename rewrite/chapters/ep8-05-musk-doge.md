# Episode 8: The Open Era

## The Oval Office

**Washington, D.C. First Trump administration.**

More than a year before the second-term rotunda seating, Musk had sat in the Oval Office opposite President Trump while the administration pressed him to locate Tesla's next gigafactory in the United States — a meeting Siddiqui reconstructs as part of Musk's long courtship of presidential power. (Siddiqui 2025, ch. 6) <!-- bib: TODO -->

The chapter is not about that meeting alone. It is about what happened when a founder who had spent the 2024 cycle campaigning personally was given a formal advisory role after the inauguration described in the previous chapter — a position concerned with spending reduction and the reorganisation of federal agencies, announced publicly and litigated almost immediately over its boundaries and conflicts.

What is structurally familiar is not wealth influencing government. Collis Huntington was more candid about buying Congress in the 1870s. What is novel is a serving executive of firms holding major federal contracts occupying a formal advisory role over the agencies that regulate them, with the conflict identified in public and not resolved in public.

Musk's path to that desk ran through PayPal's alumni network, through Tesla and SpaceX's federal contracts, and through contributions documented in Federal Election Commission filings — the same disclosure trail the rotunda chapter relied on. Vance's vice presidency and Thiel's Senate spending are part of the same circuit; this chapter follows the man who bought a communications platform outright and then sat where cameras could see him.

Official announcements described the advisory remit. Congressional correspondence and court filings disputed how far it reached. The chapter records what was established on paper and where authority was contested, without narrating events that could not be verified at draft time.

The comparison that matters for this book is visibility. An elite that once exercised influence through donations, foundations, and quiet access began exercising it in front of cameras. The relationships were not new. They had been building for two decades in filings almost nobody read. What changed was that the men on the hill no longer found it necessary to stay up there.
