# Cardinal Notes — Ambrose, Nothing Like It

The moments below are in reading order, with the earliest chapter first. More will be appended as reading continues.

- (Ch 11 (1867)) “In late May, unbelievably and unacceptably, the Chinese went on strike. ‘This is the hardest blow we have had,’ E. B. Crocker reported.”
  - Why: Labor revolt at the summit, with the boss's own words. Episode 2. [Ambrose's framing is dated; pair with Chang]
- (Ch 16) Crocker “offered a bet of $10,000 to Durant, saying that the CP would lay ten miles of track in one day.”
  - Why: A wager and a race against the clock. Episode 2.
- (Ch 17 “Done”) “Stanford swung and missed, striking only the rail. It made no difference. The telegraph operator closed the circuit and the wire went out, ‘done!’”
  - Why: The perfect cold open: the hero misses and the nation cheers anyway. Episode 2 (cold open).
