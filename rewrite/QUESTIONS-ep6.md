# Episode 6 — Research Questions

## Questions

1. What were the terms of the first Stanford Industrial Park lease to Varian, and what was Terman’s financial relationship to Varian?
2. Why did Shockley locate in Mountain View, and who were the eight who left in September 1957?
3. How did Arthur Rock and Fairchild Camera structure the Fairchild Semiconductor financing?
4. What role did Lockheed Sunnyvale and missile/space programs play in county employment and chip demand?
5. How did restrictive covenants and redlining shape who could live near the Research Park, especially East Palo Alto?
6. What contracts linked SRI to chemical warfare and Southeast Asia, and when did students learn of them?
7. What were the April Third Movement’s four demands, and what happened during the nine-day AEL occupation?
8. How did the trustees “sell” SRI in January 1970, and did classified work continue?
9. What is SRI’s own narrative of the student protests, and how should it be cited?
10. What happened on 16 May 1969 near SRI, and what contracts moved with researchers to Menlo Park?

## Answers (with sources)

1. First R&D lease to Varian in 1953; Terman investor and board member (Harris 2023, ch. 3.1; `moments.md` Ep 6; `books/cardinal/ch/4-01`).
2. Shockley chose Mountain View because his mother lived there; eight named resignees Sept. 1957 (Harris 2023, ch. 3.2; `books/cardinal/ch/4-02`).
3. Kleiner contacted Hayden, Stone; Rock proposed new company; Fairchild Camera parent with buyout option (`ch/4-02`).
4. Lockheed Missiles and Space Sunnyvale 1956, largest county employer for decades; IC early customers included Minuteman and Apollo (`ch/4-04`, `4-03`; Harris ch. 3.1 per `timeline.md`).
5. Covenants + FHA maps; Eichler exception; East Palo Alto racial/economic split (Harris 2023, ch. 3.1; `ch/4-01`).
6. CW contracts known 1965; Southeast Asia contracts 1967; picketing Menlo Park (`ch/4-07`).
7. Demands: stricter SRI guidelines, end classified work at SU+SRI, end CBW/counterinsurgency work, public trustee meeting; occupation 9–18 Apr 1969, *The Goods on AEL* (`ch/4-07`; poster quote Harris Section III frontispiece in `moments.md`).
8. Jan 1970: SRI “sold to itself,” 1% gross revenues to $25M cap; work continued off-campus (`ch/4-07`).
9. SRI history calls student claims “usually unfounded,” CW work “almost exclusively defensive” — cite as institutional position only (`ch/4-07` source note).
10. Police tear gas 16 May 1969 protesting divestment; ~$800k contracts moved AEL → SRI (`ch/4-07`).
