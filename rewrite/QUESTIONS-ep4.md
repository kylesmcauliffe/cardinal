# QUESTIONS — Episode 4: The Boy and the Farm (1884–1905)

## Questions asked
- When and where did Leland Jr. die, and what did Stanford vow afterward?
- What did the Founding Grant specify (board, land, tuition)?
- When did Muybridge photograph Stanford's trotters, and what was the outcome?
- Who was in Stanford's pioneer class on Oct 1, 1891 (Hoover)?
- How did Jane keep the university alive during probate and the $15M suit?
- What did Ross say, and what did Jordan tell George Howard on Nov 14, 1900?
- What happened with Poland Spring water in Jan 1905 and at the Moana Feb 28, 1905?

## Answers (with sources)
- Leland Jr.: typhoid, Mar 13, 1884, Florence, two months before sixteenth birthday (Harris ch 1.3; repo ch 2-12).
- Vow: "The children of California shall be our children" (Harris intro).
- Founding Grant Nov 11, 1885; opening Oct 1, 1891; ~8,000 acres; self-perpetuating board; land not to be sold (repo ch 2-12).
- Muybridge: 1878 sequential photos; horse leaves ground; Stanford patron (repo ch 2-12; Harris ch 1.3 on rehire after murder).
- Hoover: pioneer class, born 1874 (Harris ch 2.3 paraphrase).
- Probate: Stanford died Jun 21, 1893; $15M federal claim; estate won 1895/1896; Jane paid salaries via household allowance (repo ch 2-13).
- Ross: "Warning Against Coolie 'Natives' and Japanese" (Call, May 8, 1900); Jordan to Howard: "keep still" (White-J ch 17, p. 101).
- Poland Spring strychnine Jan 1905; Korea departure Feb 15 (White-J ch 28, p. 167); Moana death words (White-J ch 29, p. 173); chemists on strychnine (White-J ch 36, p. 208); Jordan "natural causes" (Examiner headline, White-J notes p. 339); mausoleum (White-J ch 45, p. 266).
