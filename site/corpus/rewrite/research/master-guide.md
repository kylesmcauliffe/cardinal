Artometrics is an independent data-science magazine and investigative publication dedicated to culture, power, and the creative economy. Our journalism is grounded in named/dated facts, primary historical source material, reproducible datasets, and structural economic analysis.

This Google Drive research library consolidates 28 foundational volumes across five interconnected thematic modules. Every module contains its source volumes alongside a dedicated Editorial Dossier & Reading Curriculum document with chapter-level questions, investigative story prompts, and analytical frameworks.

HOW TO USE THIS CORPUS FOR ARTOMETRICS EDITORIAL PRODUCTION  
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. PROPOSING REPORTS (Content OS):  
   When drafting an editorial brief (`npm run cos:brief`), writers should consult the relevant theme dossier to ground their reporting in named historical precedents, economic structures, and existing investigative literature.

2. CHART & DATA BENCHMARKING:  
   Every Artometrics report accompanies its prose with reproducible data charts. Use the historical and financial figures cited in these texts (e.g., historical land grant acreages, venture capital internal rates of return, ad-tech conversion yields, defense contract values) as benchmark series in R/Quarto analysis.

3. MAINTAINING REVERENCE FOR FACTS & OBJECTIVITY:  
   As articulated in our Style Guide (`docs/content-os/STYLE_GUIDE.md`), our tone is detached, authoritative, forensic, and analytical. We avoid cheap polemics and moralizing jargon; we let verifiable figures, primary memos, cap tables, and chronological sequences deliver the argument.
