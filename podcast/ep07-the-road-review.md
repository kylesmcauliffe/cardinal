## Changes made in review

- Cut the hook's second telling of October 3, 2002, and the inventory of the trailer, the Lego cabinet, the $336 million, and the $2.8 billion. Those scenes had already aired. The last section now names the PayPal Mafia, plants the seeing-stone and the rockets, and stops on the forward line. The last fifteen minutes are the BackRub sale, the PayPal close, and that hook.
- Patent wording: "filed in the summer of 1974" was wrong. The narration now says he began the fight that summer, and that the application which issued was filed on January 4, 1979.
- August Coppola's skybreaking sentence restored to the magazine wording. A shortened paraphrase had dropped "rather than down" and "the clouds."
- Shultz's career put in the documented order. The outline's "Stanford professor, then Bechtel president within a year" does not match the record.
- Removed an unverified 1979 *Stanford Daily* housing sentence rather than paraphrase it. Housing is carried by Henry George and the land lease.
- Removed any claim that the 1972 office at 3000 Sand Hill Road stood on university-owned land. Berlin puts the partnership in that building. Stanford's ownership of the Rosewood ground is a later, separate fact and belongs in Episode 8.
- The thesis sentence is not spoken.
