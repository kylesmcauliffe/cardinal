# Episode 6 review — The Park (1945–1969)

Narrated words: 8,846 (whitespace on speaker lines after stripping bracket cues). About 59.0 minutes at 150 wpm. Band is 8,500–9,000.

Streets sidebar (08) is 591 words, about 3.9 minutes, inside the 2–4 minute box. The last quarter is scenes: the demo (09), the occupation and the May 16 blockade (10), and the divestiture (11), about 16.8 minutes. Episode 5 was not edited. It is already in audio.

Money method, said once: Minneapolis Fed CPI, 1967 = 100, 2026 index 1004.8. Federal minimum-wage yardstick, said once: $7.25/hour, about $15,080/year, unchanged since 2009. The research note's $181 billion conversion of the 1951 defense budget was not used. That figure does not match this series' index (1951 = 78.0). The script uses the series method.

Markup matches the spike script. `hebrides` is a bed already used in Episode 5, not a second spine inside this hour. Thesis line for Episode 8 is not said.
