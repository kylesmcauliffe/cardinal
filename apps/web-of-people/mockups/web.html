A web of the people in the story

215 real figures from the Cardinal notes, drawn as bubbles that drift. Drag one, scroll or pinch to zoom, click or tap for a profile with a timeline and family history. Lines are stated relationships or two people named in the same recorded event. Nothing here is invented: where the notes are silent, the profile says so.

Bubbles are people from the Cardinal notes. A dashed ring means no verified portrait: initials instead.

How to read it

Bubbles

Size shows how many other people a person is connected to in the notes. A photo means a verified public-domain or Creative Commons portrait; a dashed ring with initials means there is none.

Lines

Heavy lines are family. Dashed lines are mentor or recruitment. Faint dotted lines mean two people appear in the same event. Place hubs group people recorded at the same place.

Status

184 of 197 events are marked Verified in the project’s ledger. The rest are labelled Draft, Needs source or Contested, always as a word.

Web of People is a prototype for Cardinal, built to be reused in Dawn.

215 people, 197 events and 13 repository files and 163 external pages as sources. Every fact cites a file in the Cardinal repository or an external page, with a verbatim quote. Where the sources are silent, this site says “not recorded”. Entries marked Draft come from drafting notes that no external source has yet confirmed. Portraits are public-domain or Creative Commons images, almost all from Wikimedia Commons, credited on each profile; where none was found the profile says so.
