A web of the people in the story

57 real figures from the Cardinal notes, drawn as bubbles that drift. Drag one, scroll to zoom, click for a profile with a timeline and family history. Lines are stated relationships or two people named in the same recorded event. Nothing here is invented: where the notes are silent, the profile says so.

Bubbles are people from the Cardinal notes. A dashed ring means no verified portrait: initials instead.

How to read it

Bubbles

Size shows how many other people a person is connected to in the notes. A photo means a verified public-domain or Creative Commons portrait; a dashed ring with initials means there is none.

Lines

Heavy lines are family. Dashed lines are mentor or recruitment. Faint dotted lines mean two people appear in the same event. Place hubs group people recorded at the same place.

Status

41 of 78 events are marked Verified in the project’s ledger. The rest are labelled Draft, Needs source or Contested, always as a word.
